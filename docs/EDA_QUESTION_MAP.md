## Hypothesis Alignment

- **H1:** EDA inspects predictor/outcome distributions, missingness, plausible signal, transformations, and dependence. It does not compare predictive performance or test H1.
- **H2:** Questions 2, 3, and 5 describe growth-startup associations overall and by sector/time. They do not establish a causal effect or formal support.
- **H3:** Question 1 summarizes startup-rate persistence and lag availability. It does not create a future gap or test reduced future-gap likelihood.
- **H4:** Question 5 compares descriptive sector distributions and associations. Formal interactions and inference are later work.

The primary RQ asks about prediction of future gaps. These questions are deliberately upstream: they establish whether measurements, panel coverage, and historical relationships make that later predictive task well-defined. The eventual target must be constructed only under the later assignment's explicit design and validation rules.
