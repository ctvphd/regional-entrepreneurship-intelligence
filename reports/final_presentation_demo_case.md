## Why selected

The case was selected programmatically from frozen dashboard and holdout artifacts using the requested storytelling criteria rather than maximum risk. It has a long observed history, sufficient comparison coverage, nonmissing predictions, moderate rather than extreme estimated risk, a recognizable regional industry, and a realized outcome that permits a clear retrospective discussion. Pittsburgh is understandable to a mixed audience without relying on personal relevance.

The live Explorer visibly confirmed the selected MSA, sector, 14 observed years, 4 comparable historical gap years, and 3 prediction records. The walkthrough is explicitly framed as a fixed historical evaluation rather than a current forecast.
